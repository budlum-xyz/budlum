## 3. The envelope and BCS

`Foreign::Aptos` holds chain id 205, a sequence number, an expiry timestamp,
the sender's Move alias and an opaque payload. The alias is
`SHA3-256(address32 || 0xBD)`. BCS is the canonical wire format: one `u8`, two
little-endian `u64`s, 32 fixed address bytes, one ULEB128 length and the
payload. `bcs_decode` refuses truncation, trailing bytes, non-canonical
ULEB128, oversized bodies and any payload above `MAX_BCS_BYTES` (TESTNET-DEFAULT,
not a measurement).

`check_envelope` applies the chain id, expiry, pinned-framework and replay
rules in that order. `MoveWorkerAdmission::admit_bcs` parses a body, checks it,
and then returns `WorkerUnavailable { door: bud_sendRawTransaction }`: a
well-formed body never reaches a fabricated Move state, and a client cannot
use the door to probe an account that does not exist. The refusal is the last
step, after the body has been validated, so the named refusal is never a
parser or an allocation door.

Named tests: `move_alias_scheme_byte_is_unused`,
`aptos_framework_commit_is_pinned`, `aptos_bcs_round_trip_is_canonical`,
`aptos_bcs_refuses_trailing_and_noncanonical_bodies`,
`uel_foreign_tx_replay_refused_aptos`,
`aptos_envelope_refuses_wrong_chain_and_expiry`,
`aptos_move_worker_refuses_by_name_after_checks`,
`uel_foreign_tx_replay_refused_at_the_move_worker`,
`aptos_move_worker_refuses_bad_bcs_before_caching`.
