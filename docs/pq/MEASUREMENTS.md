### Bounds that are stated, not derived

AIR constraints at most 4096, constraint degree at most 9, 2 opening points per column, `LogUp` interactions per row at most 16 with messages at most 8 wide. They come from reading `plonky3_air.rs` by hand and are set above the observed values; a higher bound can only lower the reported bits.

The quantum reading halves every search-type term (Fiat-Shamir challenges, grinding included) and takes the Merkle collision term as `256 / 3` bits (Brassard-Hoyer-Tapp). It follows the quadratic loss of Fiat-Shamir in the quantum random oracle model (Chiesa, Manohar, Spooner, eprint 2019/834) and is a reading of that bound, not a separate proof.
