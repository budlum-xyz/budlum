## 3. TEST-GAP-07, the method coverage ratchet

`rpc_every_method_has_success_and_refusal_test` is delivered in `2/2` together
with `.github/rpc-method-coverage-baseline.txt`. The rule, the ratchet direction
and the self-test are written there so a reader sees why a `Refused` row cannot
have a success test. Measured on this tree with that rule: 44 of the 183 rows have
both kinds of test, and 139 rows are short of at least one. Writing 139 tests does
not fit one pull request of 1 500 lines, so the ratchet records the gap, refuses a
new one, and every delivery that adds tests lowers it.
